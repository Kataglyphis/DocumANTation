% Headline paragraph for the default profile.
%
% This was commented out entirely to fit the one-pager, and the space it needs
% is real. It earns it back by being the only place on the CV that says
% something a bullet list cannot: why I work the way I do.
%
% It opened with "I studied computer science at KIT ... and now work at
% Fraunhofer IPA on real-time scene analysis" -- cut, not for space alone: the
% tagline in the header already names the employer and the role, and the
% Education section names KIT and the specializations. A summary that restates
% the page below it is the first thing a reader learns to skip.
%
% What is left is deliberately NOT "passionate about open source" -- that
% sentence is on every CV and costs nothing to write. What is claimed here is
% a body of public work a reader can open (the header carries the GitHub link,
% and the footer names the pipeline that typeset this page), plus a habit with
% a consequence: upstream, not a local patch. Naming *what* is public is what
% keeps it from being the costless version.
%
% The "fix it upstream" half deliberately overlaps the Experiences bullet on
% upstreamed fixes rather than duplicating its detail: the claim is here, the
% work is in the timeline.
%
% Two lines. A third pushes the Languages/Hobbies/Publications row onto page
% two -- rebuild and check the page count, in both languages, after any edit.
\IfLanguageName{english}{
    \par{I learned this field from other people's source code and give it back
    the same way: my renderers, my ML implementations and the pipeline that
    typeset this page are all public, and tools I depend on get fixed upstream
    rather than locally patched.}
}{
    % Nicht die Übersetzung des englischen Satzes, sondern sein Gegenstück:
    % "gebe es genauso zurück" hatte ein "es" ohne Bezugswort, und "die
    % Pipeline, die diese Seite gesetzt hat" löst als Relativsatz auf, was das
    % Englische als Attribut sagt. Beides kostete Länge und Tempo. Der
    % Schlusskontrast ("upstream statt lokal") trägt hier dieselbe Last wie
    % "rather than locally patched" drüben und darf nicht wegfallen.
    \par{Ich habe dieses Fach aus dem Quellcode anderer gelernt und gebe
    zurück, was ich bekommen habe: Meine Renderer, meine ML-Implementierungen
    und die Pipeline hinter dieser Seite sind öffentlich, und defekte Werkzeuge
    repariere ich upstream statt lokal.}
}
